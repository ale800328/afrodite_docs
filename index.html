About the App

Afrodite is a private cycle tracking app designed for women who value their health data and their privacy. Everything stays on your device — no account required, no data sent to servers, ever.

Track your cycle, understand your phases, predict upcoming periods, and care for your wellbeing — all in one place, completely offline.
