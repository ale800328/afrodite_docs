Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Afrodite is built on a simple principle: your health data belongs to you. The app collects no personal information and sends no data to any server.

Data We Collect

Afrodite does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All information you enter — cycle logs, symptoms, mood, preferences — is stored exclusively on your device using encrypted local storage.

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device using AES-256 encrypted storage.
  • No data is ever uploaded to a server, cloud, or third-party service.
  • Deleting the app removes all data permanently.

Third-Party Services

  • RevenueCat — used to process premium subscriptions. RevenueCat may collect purchase history and device identifiers as required for payment processing. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.
  • Apple HealthKit — optionally used to read sleep data for pre-filling your daily log. No data is written to HealthKit without your explicit consent.

Authentication

The app uses a local PIN and/or biometrics (Face ID / Touch ID) to protect access. These credentials never leave your device.

Children's Privacy

Afrodite is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
