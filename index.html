See it in action

Everything you need to understand your cycle, in one beautiful app.

Home

Know where you are in your cycle, every day

The home screen shows your current phase, cycle day, and days until your next period — at a glance. Hormone indicators (E2 and PG) help you understand what's happening in your body right now.

Predictions

See your next period, fertile window & phases ahead

Afrodite calculates your cycle phase map and fertile window based on your personal history — not a generic 28-day average. The more you log, the more accurate it gets.

Daily Log

Track how you feel — mood, symptoms, sleep & more

Log your flow, mood, physical symptoms, energy, sleep, temperature, and notes in one tap. Everything is stored locally and tied to your cycle — so patterns emerge over time.

Analytics

Your body, decoded

The Analytics screen calculates your Rhythm Score, average cycle length, luteal phase duration, and more — building a picture of your hormonal health that evolves with every cycle logged.

Sanctuary

Guided practices matched to your phase

Breathing for cramps. Affirmations for the luteal phase. Movement for your period days. The Sanctuary adapts to where you are in your cycle — so the right support is always one tap away.

Cycle Report

A full report of your cycle history

Get a detailed analysis covering cycle regularity, flow patterns, mood trends, sleep, and a prediction for your next cycle — based on your own data, not population averages.
